- Initial release for Minecraft 1.21.11
- Explore structures for Level I Efficiency, Unbreaking, and Protection books
- Themed structures add extra enchants: Swift Sneak in ancient cities, Soul Speed in fortresses and bastions, Respiration and Depth Strider in shipwrecks and underwater ruins
- Fortune I comes from buried treasure
- Mending is a separate rare roll on late-game structures (about 0.75 books per full clear for most sites; woodland mansion about 1.25; nether fortress about 0.5; a full ancient city about 1.5 including ice boxes)
- Silk Touch and Feather Falling stay on villagers; End loot is unchanged
